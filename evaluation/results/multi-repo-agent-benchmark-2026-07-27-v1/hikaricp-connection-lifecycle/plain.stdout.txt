Repository investigation only; no files, generated sources, or build outputs were modified, and no generator or test suite was run.

1. Normal end-to-end lifecycle

Verified current behavior

1) Public entry and pool startup

- `HikariDataSource.getConnection()` first rejects calls after datasource closure. An eagerly constructed datasource takes `fastPathPool`; a default-constructed datasource lazily validates configuration, constructs one `HikariPool` under double-checked locking, seals configuration, and delegates to `HikariPool.getConnection()` (`HikariDataSource.getConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariDataSource.java:92-128`).
- The eager constructor validates and copies configuration, constructs `HikariPool`, and seals the datasource immediately (`HikariDataSource(HikariConfig)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariDataSource.java:74-84`).
- `PoolBase` snapshots connection defaults, timeout values, validation mode, exception override, credentials provider, and underlying datasource strategy during pool construction (`PoolBase.PoolBase`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:97-122`; `PoolBase.initializeDataSource`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:321-353`).
- `HikariPool` creates the `ConcurrentBag`, suspension lock, housekeeping service, runs fail-fast initialization, installs metrics/health/JMX, creates single-worker add/close executors, creates the leak-task factory, and schedules the housekeeper (`HikariPool.HikariPool`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:88-131`; `UtilityElf.createThreadPoolExecutor`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/UtilityElf.java:168-190`).

2) Acquisition and pool growth

- `HikariPool.getConnection()` acquires the suspension gate, records the start timestamp, and calls `connectionBag.borrow(timeout, MILLISECONDS)` (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:152-160`).
- If no entry is immediately available, `ConcurrentBag.borrow()` increments `waiters`, asks its listener—`HikariPool.addBagItem()`—for growth, and waits on the fair synchronous handoff queue (`ConcurrentBag.borrow`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:161-204`; `HikariPool.addBagItem`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:339-345`).
- The connection-adder’s `PoolEntryCreator` creates while the pool is normal, below maximum size, and either below minimum idle or has more waiters than idle entries. Failed creation retries with exponential backoff from 10 ms up to 5 seconds (`HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.call`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:745-776`; `HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.shouldContinueCreating`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:778-787`).
- Creation flows through `createPoolEntry()` → `newPoolEntry()` → `newConnection()`. The datasource supplies the physical connection, `setupConnection()` establishes configured state, and the resulting `PoolEntry` is added to the bag (`HikariPool.createPoolEntry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:481-520`; `PoolBase.newPoolEntry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:208-211`; `PoolBase.newConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:360-408`; `HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.call`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:751-758`).
- Physical setup temporarily applies the validation network timeout, normalizes read-only/auto-commit/isolation/catalog/schema, checks validation support, runs connection-init SQL, and restores the original network timeout (`PoolBase.setupConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:416-456`; `PoolBase.checkValidationSupport`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:479-493`).

3) Validation, proxy creation, and delivery

- Once borrowed, an entry is rejected if already marked evicted. Otherwise, it is validated only when its `lastAccessed` age exceeds the default 500 ms alive-bypass window; dead or evicted entries are physically retired and the acquisition loop continues with the remaining budget (`HikariPool.aliveBypassWindowMs`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:62-68`; `HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:165-181`).
- A successful borrow records metrics, optionally calls JDBC `beginRequest()`, schedules leak detection, and returns a proxy (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:171-180`).
- `PoolEntry.createProxyConnection()` passes the physical connection, statement-tracking list, leak task, and default state to `ProxyFactory` (`PoolEntry.createProxyConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:99-102`).
- At build time, `JavassistProxyFactory` injects `HikariProxyConnection` construction into `ProxyFactory` and generates JDBC delegates that route thrown `SQLException`s through `checkException()` (`JavassistProxyFactory.main`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/JavassistProxyFactory.java:47-75`; `JavassistProxyFactory.modifyProxyFactory`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/JavassistProxyFactory.java:77-109`; `JavassistProxyFactory.generateProxyClass`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/JavassistProxyFactory.java:114-185`). The checked-in `ProxyFactory` bodies are deliberate build-time placeholders, not the runtime implementation (`ProxyFactory.getProxyConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyFactory.java:36-50`).

4) Application use and close

- Statements created through the proxy are tracked and wrapped, allowing automatic cleanup on connection close (`ProxyConnection.trackStatement`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:205-210`; `ProxyConnection.createStatement`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:281-300`; `ProxyConnection.prepareStatement`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:324-364`).
- Proxy setters maintain dirty bits for read-only, auto-commit, isolation, catalog, network timeout, and schema (`ProxyConnection` dirty-bit declarations, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:39-65`; setters, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:408-510`).
- `ProxyConnection.close()` closes outstanding statements, cancels leak detection, rolls back dirty non-auto-commit work, restores dirty connection state, clears warnings, switches the proxy delegate to the closed sentinel, and recycles the `PoolEntry` in a `finally` block (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:238-271`).
- Resetting only touches properties whose dirty bit is set and whose proxy value differs from the pool default (`PoolBase.resetConnectionState`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:213-251`).
- `PoolEntry.recycle()` updates `lastAccessed` and delegates to the pool unless shutdown or another path has already nulled the physical connection (`PoolEntry.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:74-83`).
- `HikariPool.recycle()` records usage, physically closes an evicted entry, or optionally calls JDBC `endRequest()` and requites the entry into the bag (`HikariPool.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:428-449`).
- Thus ordinary application `Connection.close()` returns ownership to the pool; it does not normally close the physical connection (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:238-271`; `HikariPool.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:428-449`).

2. Acquisition-loop timing and timeout semantics

Verified current behavior

- The timeout clock starts after `suspendResumeLock.acquire()`. Time spent blocked by pool suspension is therefore outside `connectionTimeout`; normal suspension can block indefinitely, while `com.zaxxer.hikari.throwIfSuspended=true` produces `SQLTransientException` instead (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:152-159`; `SuspendResumeLock.acquire`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/SuspendResumeLock.java:63-73`).
- `ConcurrentBag.borrow()` converts the supplied timeout to nanoseconds only after thread-local and shared-list scans. Each failed handoff candidate subtracts elapsed poll time and the loop stops when at most 10,000 ns remain (`ConcurrentBag.borrow`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:161-200`).
- The outer acquisition loop initially gives the bag the full hard timeout. It recomputes `hardTimeout - elapsedMillis(startTime)` only after finding an evicted or dead entry (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:158-181`).
- Consequently, the timeout is a wait/retry budget rather than a strict wall-clock deadline around every operation: validation occurs after a successful bag borrow and a live result is returned without a post-validation deadline check; slow validation can therefore make total latency exceed `hardTimeout` (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:160-180`; `PoolBase.isConnectionDead`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:157-192`).
- Physical creation happens asynchronously in the adder. A caller can time out while datasource connection creation remains in progress; a completed connection is subsequently added if creation had already begun (`HikariPool.addBagItem`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:339-345`; `HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.call`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:745-765`).
- Timeout records acquisition-duration metrics, logs pool state, increments the timeout metric, and throws `SQLTransientConnectionException` containing elapsed time and total/active/idle/waiting counts. The most recent creation/validation failure is attached as cause and, when it is a `SQLException`, as `nextException` with copied SQL state/error code (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:181-184`; `HikariPool.createTimeoutException`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:688-721`).
- Interruption of the bag wait restores the thread’s interrupt status and becomes a plain `SQLException`; the suspension permit is always released (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:186-192`).
- Configuration defaults are 30-second acquisition timeout and 5-second validation timeout; zero acquisition timeout maps to `Integer.MAX_VALUE`, and both configured timeouts have a default 250 ms floor (`HikariConfig` defaults, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:49-56`; `HikariConfig.setConnectionTimeout`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:182-200`; `HikariConfig.setValidationTimeout`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:342-358`).

3. ConcurrentBag coordination and growth

Verified current behavior

- Bag states are `NOT_IN_USE`, `IN_USE`, `REMOVED`, and `RESERVED`, transitioned atomically by each `PoolEntry` (`ConcurrentBag.IConcurrentBagEntry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:104-120`; `PoolEntry.compareAndSet`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:150-173`).
- Borrow order is thread-local LIFO first, then CAS scanning of the shared list, then the fair `SynchronousQueue` handoff (`ConcurrentBag.ConcurrentBag`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:139-150`; `ConcurrentBag.borrow`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:161-200`).
- A shared-list borrower that may have stolen another waiter’s connection requests capacity for the remaining waiters. A borrower that finds nothing requests capacity for the current waiter count (`ConcurrentBag.borrow`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:174-188`).
- `HikariPool.addBagItem()` submits one adder task only when waiter count exceeds already queued add tasks, limiting duplicate queued growth requests (`HikariPool.addBagItem`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:339-345`).
- Newly added, requited, or unreserved entries spin/yield and directly offer to waiting borrowers before falling back to thread-local caching. Virtual threads use short parks rather than repeated yielding (`ConcurrentBag.add`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:240-257`; `ConcurrentBag.requite`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:216-237`; `ConcurrentBag.unreserve`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:347-368`).
- Requite sets `NOT_IN_USE`; if no waiter accepts the entry, at most 16 references are cached per thread, optionally weak depending on classloader or system-property policy (`ConcurrentBag.requite`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:216-237`; `ConcurrentBag.useWeakThreadLocals`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:425-444`).
- `fillPool()` separately maintains `minimumIdle`. It submits enough creator tasks to close the observed idle deficit when below `maximumPoolSize`; creator-side `shouldContinueCreating()` rechecks current need and prevents queued tasks from deliberately overfilling (`HikariPool.fillPool`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:522-538`; `HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.shouldContinueCreating`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:778-787`).

4. Validation, dead connections, bypass, and exceptions

Verified current behavior

- Connections reused within 500 ms of `lastAccessed` bypass liveness validation unless marked evicted. The window is overrideable through the `com.zaxxer.hikari.aliveBypassWindowMs` system property (`HikariPool.aliveBypassWindowMs`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:62-63`; `HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:165-180`).
- Validation applies `validationTimeout` as network timeout and uses JDBC4 `Connection.isValid()` unless a `connectionTestQuery` is configured. For query validation without network-timeout support it attempts statement query timeout; it then restores the original network timeout and optionally rolls back isolated internal work (`PoolBase.isConnectionDead`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:157-192`).
- Validation timeout is rounded to at least one second for `isValid()`/query timeout. Drivers without network-timeout support cannot honor subsecond or fractional-second validation precisely, and the pool logs that limitation (`PoolBase.isConnectionDead`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:160-174`; `PoolBase.getAndSetNetworkTimeout`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:547-572`).
- Any validation exception stores `lastConnectionFailure`, logs a warning, and classifies the connection as dead (`PoolBase.isConnectionDead`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:184-191`).
- Creation/setup failures close a partially created physical connection, preserve the failure, and are retried by the adder. `ConnectionSetupException` during fail-fast is treated as immediately fatal (`PoolBase.newConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:360-407`; `HikariPool.createPoolEntry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:485-520`; `HikariPool.checkFailFast`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:567-599`).
- Runtime proxy exceptions are examined through at most ten linked SQLExceptions. SQLState class `08`, listed fatal states/codes, or `SQLExceptionOverride.MUST_EVICT` marks the connection broken; `DO_NOT_EVICT` suppresses that decision (`ProxyConnection.checkException`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:150-186`).
- Broken-connection handling logs the decisive state/code, cancels leak detection, evicts the `PoolEntry`, and replaces the proxy delegate with the closed sentinel (`ProxyConnection.checkException`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:176-183`).
- Failure while closing tracked statements also cancels the leak task, evicts the entry, and closes the proxy delegate (`ProxyConnection.closeStatements`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:212-232`).
- Failure during rollback/reset/clearWarnings is passed through `checkException()` unless the entry was already evicted; recycling still occurs in `finally` (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:245-269`).

5. Proxy ownership, close, recycle, and state reset

Verified current behavior

- While checked out, the proxy owns application-facing lifecycle and the `PoolEntry` remains `IN_USE`. Closing the proxy is idempotent because subsequent calls see `ClosedConnection.CLOSED_CONNECTION` (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:238-271`; `ProxyConnection.ClosedConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:538-563`).
- Dirty transactional work is tracked only while auto-commit is disabled. Commit/full rollback clears the dirty flag; statement and metadata operations can mark it dirty (`ProxyConnection.markCommitStateDirty`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:193-198`; `ProxyConnection.commit/rollback`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:374-396`; generated delegation policy, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/JavassistProxyFactory.java:157-180`).
- State restoration occurs before the entry is made available to another borrower. The reset covers read-only, auto-commit, isolation, catalog, network timeout, and schema, but only for dirty and changed properties (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:248-269`; `PoolBase.resetConnectionState`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:213-251`).
- If retirement previously nulled the `PoolEntry.connection`, a later application close still closes the proxy logically but `PoolEntry.recycle()` does nothing, preventing the already-retired entry from returning to the bag (`PoolEntry.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:175-192`; `PoolEntry.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:74-83`).

6. Leak detection

Verified current behavior

- Leak detection is scheduled only after a connection passes acquisition validation and immediately before proxy construction. A threshold of zero returns a no-op singleton (`HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:171-180`; `ProxyLeakTaskFactory.schedule`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyLeakTaskFactory.java:38-53`).
- The task captures the borrowing thread, physical connection name, and allocation stack; after the threshold it logs an apparent leak (`ProxyLeakTask.ProxyLeakTask`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyLeakTask.java:57-71`; `ProxyLeakTask.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyLeakTask.java:73-86`).
- Normal proxy close, explicit user eviction, broken-connection detection, and statement-cleanup failure cancel the leak task (`ProxyConnection.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:245-247`; `HikariPool.evictConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:262-269`; `ProxyConnection.checkException`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:176-183`; `ProxyConnection.closeStatements`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyConnection.java:221-227`).
- Cancellation after a leak has already fired logs that the connection was returned (“unleaked”) (`ProxyLeakTask.cancel`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/ProxyLeakTask.java:88-94`).
- Normal configuration validation disables leak detection below two seconds or above a nonzero `maxLifetime`; unit-test mode bypasses that restriction (`HikariConfig.validateNumerics`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:1120-1125`).
- Shutdown has no direct handle to cancel each active proxy’s leak task. Internal housekeeping shutdown cancels queued scheduler work, but a user-supplied scheduled executor is intentionally not shut down; this is a source-verified edge requiring a focused test for an active borrowed connection (`HikariPool.shutdown`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:212-245`; `HikariPool.destroyHouseKeepingExecutorService`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:658-666`; `HikariPool.abortActiveConnections`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:545-559`).

7. Recycle, eviction, retirement, and physical close

Verified current behavior

- Soft eviction marks the entry first. An owner or a caller that successfully reserves an idle entry closes it immediately; an active entry remains marked and is closed on application recycle or the next acquisition attempt (`HikariPool.softEvictConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:613-635`; `HikariPool.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:433-448`; `HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:165-169`).
- Public `HikariDataSource.evictConnection()` accepts only an open Hikari proxy, cancels its leak task, and treats an unclosed proxy as the owner, making eviction immediate (`HikariDataSource.evictConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariDataSource.java:321-334`; `HikariPool.evictConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:257-273`).
- Each newly created entry receives a randomized max-lifetime task and, when enabled, a randomized periodic keepalive task (`HikariPool.createPoolEntry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:481-506`).
- Max-lifetime performs soft eviction. If the entry was idle and closed immediately, it also requests growth for current waiters; active entries are merely marked until returned (`HikariPool.MaxLifetimeTask.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:853-868`; `HikariPool.softEvictConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:626-635`).
- Keepalive operates only if it can reserve an idle entry. It validates and either evicts/refills a dead connection or unreserves a live connection for handoff (`HikariPool.KeepaliveTask.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:870-892`).
- Housekeeper idle retirement applies only when `idleTimeout > 0` and `minimumIdle < maximumPoolSize`. It examines idle entries, keeps at least `minimumIdle`, requires successful reservation, and closes entries older than `idleTimeout` (`HikariPool.HouseKeeper.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:812-845`).
- `closeConnection()` removes the borrowed/reserved entry from the bag, calls `PoolEntry.close()` to cancel max-lifetime/keepalive and detach the raw connection, then asynchronously invokes physical close. If the pool remains normal, it calls `fillPool()` afterward (`HikariPool.closeConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:451-468`; `PoolEntry.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolEntry.java:175-192`).
- Physical close logs the reason, attempts a 15-second network timeout, invokes the raw JDBC `close()` through try-with-resources, and suppresses close failures (`PoolBase.quietlyCloseConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:137-155`).

8. Maintenance, shutdown, configuration, and observability

Verified current behavior

- The housekeeper runs after an initial 100 ms delay and then at a fixed delay, defaulting to 30 seconds. Each run refreshes runtime acquisition timeout, validation timeout, leak threshold, and catalog, performs clock anomaly checks, retires idle entries, logs pool state, and fills minimum idle (`HikariPool.HikariPool`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:116-119`; `HikariPool.HouseKeeper.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:793-850`).
- Retrograde clock movement soft-evicts every connection; forward leap/thread starvation only logs because it naturally accelerates retirement (`HikariPool.HouseKeeper.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:815-828`).
- Acquisition-driven growth and housekeeper fill share the same adder executor. The former responds to waiters; the latter targets minimum idle. Creator-side current-state checks arbitrate stale queued work (`HikariPool.addBagItem`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:339-345`; `HikariPool.fillPool`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:522-538`; `HikariPool.PoolEntryCreator.shouldContinueCreating`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:778-787`).
- `HikariDataSource.close()` atomically becomes idempotently closed, delegates to pool shutdown, and restores interrupt status if shutdown is interrupted (`HikariDataSource.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariDataSource.java:337-358`).
- Pool shutdown sets `POOL_SHUTDOWN`, cancels the housekeeper, soft-evicts, stops creation, destroys the internal scheduler, closes the bag to additions, repeatedly aborts active connections and re-evicts for up to ten seconds, then stops network-timeout and physical-close executors (`HikariPool.shutdown`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:201-255`).
- Active shutdown first detaches the raw connection, attempts `Connection.abort()` on an assassin executor, falls back to physical close on any failure, and removes the entry from the bag (`HikariPool.abortActiveConnections`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:540-559`).
- Bag closure prevents future adds but does not itself signal current synchronous-queue waiters; callers already in `borrow()` remain governed by their timeout or interruption (`ConcurrentBag.close`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:285-292`; `ConcurrentBag.borrow`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/util/ConcurrentBag.java:189-204`).
- Runtime-changeable configuration includes catalog, acquisition/validation/idle/leak/max-lifetime values, and pool-size limits; other configuration is sealed after startup (`HikariConfig` fields, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:60-104`; `HikariConfig.checkIfSealed`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:1154-1157`). Acquisition timeout, validation timeout, leak threshold, and catalog take effect when housekeeper refreshes them (`HikariPool.HouseKeeper.run`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:802-810`).
- Numeric validation normalizes invalid max-lifetime, keepalive, leak, timeout, pool-size, and idle-timeout combinations before startup (`HikariConfig.validateNumerics`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariConfig.java:1101-1152`).
- Metrics expose physical creation duration, successful/failed acquisition duration, usage duration, and timeout count. Successful borrow also sets `PoolEntry.lastBorrowed`, which drives usage duration (`PoolBase.IMetricsTrackerDelegate`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:720-734`; `PoolBase.MetricsTrackerDelegate`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:741-785`; `IMetricsTracker`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/metrics/IMetricsTracker.java:22-33`).
- Pool metrics/JMX stats report pending, idle, total, active, max, and minimum counts; MXBean values are explicitly point-in-time and transient (`HikariPool.getPoolStats`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:668-686`; `HikariPoolMXBean`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/HikariPoolMXBean.java:26-72`).
- JMX registration publishes pool configuration and pool management beans and unregisters them during shutdown (`PoolBase.handleMBeans`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:270-312`; `HikariPool.shutdown`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:250-254`).
- Metric registries select Dropwizard 4/5 or Micrometer trackers by runtime type; a custom tracker factory is also supported (`HikariPool.setMetricRegistry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:275-313`).
- Optional health registration reflectively installs Codahale health checks (`HikariPool.setHealthCheckRegistry`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:315-333`).
- Debug logging exposes creation/setup outcomes, pool counts, timeout state, reset properties, closure reasons, and connection-creation backoff (`PoolBase.newConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:371-405`; `HikariPool.logPoolState`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:419-425`; `HikariPool.createTimeoutException`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:700-720`; `PoolBase.resetConnectionState`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:248-250`; `PoolBase.quietlyCloseConnection`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/PoolBase.java:137-153`).
- The optional `com.zaxxer.hikari.enableRequestBoundaries` hook surrounds successful checkout/recycle with JDBC `beginRequest()`/`endRequest()`; failures are warnings and do not fail acquisition or recycle (`HikariPool.isRequestBoundariesEnabled`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:65-65`; `HikariPool.getConnection(long)`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:171-179`; `HikariPool.recycle`, `src/main/java/com/zaxxer/hikari/pool/HikariPool.java:440-447`).
